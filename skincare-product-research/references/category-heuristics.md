# Category-Specific Heuristics

Formulation-analysis heuristics by product category for the `skincare-product-research` skill. Load this file during Step A3 (identify the functional architecture) and when comparing products within one category.

## Cleansers

Never equate stronger degreasing or more foam with better cleansing.

Examine:

- soap vs syndet;
- anionic / amphoteric / nonionic surfactants;
- fatty acid + KOH / NaOH soap formation;
- pH;
- likely lipid extraction;
- post-wash tightness risk;
- cleansing requirement based on sunscreen / makeup use;
- rinse-off nature of claimed actives.

Treat botanical extracts, hyaluronic acid, proteins, niacinamide, and similar rinse-off ingredients as secondary unless evidence shows meaningful retained benefit.

## Moisturizers

Think in terms of:

- humectancy;
- emolliency;
- occlusion;
- barrier lipids;
- irritation / fragrance;
- sensory finish.

Do not assume "ceramide-containing" automatically means superior barrier repair. Formulation and lipid organization matter.

## Sunscreens

Protection and adequate real-world application take priority over luxury ingredients.

Evaluate:

- broad-spectrum protection;
- SPF;
- UVA protection metric when available;
- filter system;
- photostability;
- water resistance;
- film formation;
- usability at adequate application quantity;
- finish and eye comfort.

## Retinoids and acids

Distinguish cosmetics from medicines.

For retinoids, separate:

- retinyl esters;
- retinol;
- retinal / retinaldehyde;
- retinoic acid / tretinoin;
- synthetic retinoids such as adapalene.

For acids, distinguish:

- AHA;
- salicylic acid / BHA in skincare usage;
- PHA — Polyhydroxy Acid;
- cosmetic exfoliation from medical treatment.

Discuss irritation burden and interaction with the user's other actives.
